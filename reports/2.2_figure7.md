# Step 2.2 — Figure 7 vs paper (external validation)

> **Status: in progress.** This runs on the provisional recipe from step 1.3. Excluding references above 47 wt%, the max relative error is 13.2% for method a and 29.8% for method c. Both miss the paper's bound of ≤ 8–9%. The t-test check below also passes for recipes known to be wrong, so it does not confirm reproduction.

Each method is fit on **all 12 calibration discs** (winning preprocessing re-derived by the same search as 1.3) and predicts the paper's **8 Figure-7 points** (V1–V3 measured in two series each → 6 points; V4 & V5, the precision discs at 48 & 20 wt%, each the mean of 6 series; V6 below the calibration range and V7 the optimization sample are excluded). Unlike 1.3 this is descriptive, not a gate: the headline is the **cal→val gap** (RMSEP − RMSECV) — how much error the honest external test adds over the optimistic in-sample CV.

| Method | Winner | RMSECV (cal) | RMSEP (val) | gap | R²_ext | bias | p (ours) | p (paper) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a | MSC | 1.36 | 3.90 | +2.54 | 0.842 | -0.17 | 0.914 | 0.526 |
| b | SNV | 0.72 | 5.75 | +5.03 | 0.657 | -2.02 | 0.355 | 0.241 |
| c | MMN | 1.48 | 5.78 | +4.30 | 0.653 | -2.51 | 0.243 | 0.730 |

RMSECV (cal) is in wt%; RMSEP (val) in wt%; bias = mean(pred − ref), +ve = systematic over-estimate. Relative-error envelope and the trend (under mid-range, over above ~47 wt%) are in `2.2_figure7.png`.

**External validation: the gap is the point.** RMSEP runs 3.9–5.8 wt% versus RMSECV 0.7–1.5 wt% — a +5.0 wt% jump at worst (method b), the optimism of in-sample CV at n≈11 made visible. All three paired t-tests give p > 0.05 → **no significant difference from the TG-FTIR reference**, the same conclusion as the paper (exact p differs). This check also passes for recipes known to be wrong.
